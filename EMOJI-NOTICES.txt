Messaggi — emoji image assets

Data and source: emoji-datasource-apple 16.0.0, iamcal/emoji-data.
https://github.com/iamcal/emoji-data/tree/v16.0.0
https://www.npmjs.com/package/emoji-datasource-apple/v/16.0.0

The compact lookup table is derived from emoji.json. The local WebP sheet
is an optimized conversion of img/apple/sheets-256/32.png. Only entries
explicitly marked has_img_apple are rendered with this sheet. Unsupported
sequences retain their Unicode text. No message content is sent to a CDN.
